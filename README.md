# Awesome-Music-Streaming-Audio-Player

## Top Music Streaming & Audio Player Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Self-Hosted Music Streaming Servers, Audiophile Desktop/Mobile Players, Subsonic/Jellyfin Clients & Bit-Perfect Audio Engines*  

**Last updated: November 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Music Streaming & Audio Players**. These tools empower audiophiles, music collectors, and developers to stream lossless FLAC/ALAC audio, self-host private Spotify/Tidal-style music clouds, play local hi-res music libraries with bit-perfect DAC output, and build web/mobile audio players.



**Examples** include Spotify Web, SoundCloud, Tidal, Deezer, Apple Music Web, Amazon Music, Pandora, YouTube Music, Qobuz, Audiomack, and `ernani1234/musike` (the category leaders). 



**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosted music streaming servers (`Navidrome`, `Funkwhale`, `Jellyfin`), audiophile desktop players (`Strawberry`, `Clementine`, `Tauon`, `DeaDBeeF`), mobile Subsonic/YouTube Music clients (`Symfonium`-style / `InnerTune` / `Harmony Music`), and terminal music players (`MPD`, `cmus`) — ideal for audiophiles, self-hosters, music archivists, and audio developers.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Spotify Web Player](https://spotify.com)**  

  Global cloud audio streaming platform offering algorithmic playlists, podcasts, lossless/high-bitrate streaming, and Spotify Connect device handoff.



- **[Apple Music Web](https://apple.com)**  

  Browser-based and native streaming platform featuring lossless ALAC up to 24-bit/192kHz, Spatial Audio (Dolby Atmos), and iCloud Music Library matching.



- **[Tidal](https://tidal.com)**  

  Audiophile-focused cloud streaming service offering HiRes FLAC (up to 24-bit/192kHz), Dolby Atmos, direct artist payouts, and DJ software integration.



- **[Qobuz](https://qobuz.com)**  

  High-resolution streaming and digital download store specializing in uncompressed 24-bit FLAC albums, digital liner notes, and editorial curation.



- **[YouTube Music](https://youtube.com)**  

  Cloud music streaming platform combining official studio releases with live performances, remixes, and user-uploaded audio from YouTube.



- **[SoundCloud](https://soundcloud.com)**  

  Creator-first audio distribution and streaming platform famed for independent artists, DJ mixes, stems, and waveform-timed comments.



- **[Deezer](https://deezer.com)**  

  Global music streaming service featuring HiFi lossless FLAC audio, Flow personalized radio, and built-in SongCatcher audio recognition.



- **[Amazon Music Web](https://amazon.com)**  

  Cloud music streaming service offering HD/Ultra HD lossless audio, spatial audio tracks, and Alexa multi-room playback.



- **[Pandora](https://pandora.com)**  

  Personalized internet radio and on-demand streaming platform powered by the Music Genome Project's acoustic attribute taxonomy.



- **[Audiomack](https://audiomack.com)**  

  Creator-friendly music streaming and discovery platform emphasizing hip-hop, Afrobeats, electronic, and independent artist uploads.





## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[ernani1234/musike](https://github.com)**  

  Open-source music streaming and audio player application engineered for clean playback, library organization, and modern audio listening workflows.



- **[Navidrome](https://github.com)**  

  Modern, ultra-fast open-source personal music streaming server written in Go, supporting massive libraries, multi-user accounts, transcoding, and Subsonic/OpenSubsonic APIs.



- **[Funkwhale](https://funkwhale.audio)**  

  Decentralized, federated open-source audio platform (an open alternative to SoundCloud and Spotify) powered by ActivityPub for self-hosting music, podcasts, and channels.



- **[Jellyfin (Music & Audio Streaming)](https://github.com)**  

  Volunteer-built open-source media system serving lossless music libraries, smart playlists, and lyrics to desktop, web, and dedicated mobile music apps like **[Finamp](https://github.com)**.



- **[Koel](https://github.com)**  

  Sleek, web-based personal audio streaming service written in Vue and Laravel featuring a modern Spotify-like interface, smart playlists, and S3 storage support.



- **[Ampache](https://github.com)**  

  Veteran open-source web-based audio/video streaming application and file manager supporting Subsonic, UPnP/DLNA, WebDAV, and Plex APIs.



- **[AzuraCast](https://github.com)**  

  Self-hosted, all-in-one open-source web radio management suite enabling anyone to run live Icecast/SHOUTcast internet radio stations with AutoDJ.



- **[Strawberry Music Player](https://github.com)**  

  Audiophile-grade open-source cross-platform music player and collection organizer (forked from Clementine) supporting bit-perfect ALSA/JACK/WASAPI output, Tidal/Qobuz integration, and ReplayGain.



- **[Tauon Music Box](https://github.com)**  

  Streamlined, modern open-source desktop music player for Linux and Windows featuring CUE sheet support, synced lyrics, Last.fm/ListenBrainz scrobbling, and Subsonic/Koel streaming.



- **[DeaDBeeF](https://github.com)**  

  Lightweight, modular open-source audiophile music player for Linux, macOS, and BSD supporting gapless playback, 18-band EQ, DSD/FLAC/APE, and custom layouts.



- **[Audacious](https://github.com)**  

  Fast, low-resource open-source audio player with Winamp Classic skin compatibility, GTK/Qt interfaces, and high-precision DSP plugins.



- **[MPD (Music Player Daemon)](https://github.com)**  

  Flexible, server-side open-source audio playback daemon controllable over the network via CLI (`ncmpcpp`), mobile apps, or web frontends (`ympd`/`myMPD`).



- **[cmus (C* Music Player)](https://github.com)**  

  Small, blazing-fast open-source ncurses console music player for Unix-like systems handling tens of thousands of tracks instantaneously with Vim-style keybindings.



- **[Feishin](https://github.com)**  

  Modern, Spotify-inspired open-source desktop and web music client built for Navidrome, Jellyfin, and OpenSubsonic servers with synced lyrics and smart playlists.



- **[Sonixd / Supersonic](https://github.com)**  

  Lightweight, native open-source desktop Subsonic/Jellyfin client written in Go featuring gapless playback, ReplayGain, and low RAM usage.



- **[Harmony Music](https://github.com)**  

  Cross-platform open-source Flutter music streaming client providing ad-free YouTube Music streaming, offline caching, synced lyrics, and dynamic theming.



- **[Nuclear](https://github.com)**  

  Free, open-source desktop music player that aggregates and streams audio from multiple free sources (YouTube, Jamendo, SoundCloud, Audius) with Last.fm scrobbling.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **[Spotube](https://github.com)** for an open-source, cross-platform music client pairing Spotify playlists/recommendations with YouTube/JioSaavn/Piped audio streams.

- **[InnerTune / RiMusic / Metrolist](https://github.com)** for Material You open-source Android YouTube Music clients with background play and offline downloads.

- **[Mopidy](https://github.com)** and **[Volumio / Moode Audio](https://github.com)** for open-source Raspberry Pi audiophile network music players and multi-room streaming hubs.

- **[Howler.js](https://github.com)** and **[AmplitudeJS](https://github.com)** for open-source Web Audio API libraries enabling developers to build custom HTML5 web audio players.

- Many community **Subsonic iOS/Android clients (`Tempo`, `Subtracks`, `Amperfy`)**, **FFmpeg/SoX lossless transcoding scripts**, and **ReplayGain (`rsgain`) loudness normalizers**.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **Navidrome** or **Jellyfin**, **Feishin / Supersonic** (desktop/web UI), **Finamp / Tempo** (mobile), and **Strawberry / MPD** (bit-perfect local playback) with **ernani1234/musike** for a sovereign, subscription-free music streaming ecosystem.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Third-party streaming clients that scrape or proxy commercial music catalogs (e.g., YouTube Music or Spotify wrappers) may break when upstream APIs change and can violate provider Terms of Service; self-hosting legally owned digital music libraries (`FLAC`, `ALAC`, `MP3`, `Ogg Opus`) on personal servers like Navidrome or Jellyfin provides the most reliable, lawful experience.

- Always secure self-hosted streaming servers exposed over the internet behind HTTPS/TLS reverse proxies and strong user authentication.
